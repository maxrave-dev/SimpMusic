# SimpMusic 2.6.0

## New
- **Library sync between your phone and your computer**: pair the Android app with the Desktop app once by scanning a QR code under Settings → Library sync. Likes, followed artists, saved albums and podcasts, playlists and the last year of listening history then stay the same on both: by themselves when SimpMusic opens on the phone, every 15 minutes while it is open on the computer, and on Sync now. It runs over your own Wi-Fi or Tailscale, downloads and device settings stay on each device, and once paired the phone can send its sign-ins to the computer without scanning a code again. ([#2663](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2663))
- **SoundCloud Now Playing style**: a fourth style under Settings → Interface → Now Playing style. The artwork fills a card, the song's waveform scrolls past the playhead and is the seek bar, comments that name a moment appear over it, and a comment box with quick reactions sits underneath. ([#2847](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2847))
- **8D audio**: a switch under Settings → Playback moves the song around your head, with the rotation direction and the time per revolution (3 to 30 seconds) of your choice, on Android and Desktop. ([#2846](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2846))
- **Import sign-ins from a browser** (Desktop): Log in to YouTube and Log in to Spotify can take the sign-in from a browser profile on the same computer instead of pasting cookies by hand. Only that service's cookies are read and the browser is not changed. It reads Chromium-based and Firefox-based browsers on macOS and Linux, Safari on macOS, and Firefox-based browsers on Windows. ([#2850](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2850))
- **Word-by-word lyrics from LRCLIB**: lyrics from LRCLIB light up word by word when LRCLIB has word timing for the song. ([#2851](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2851))
- **Music app colours**: the app colour picker in Settings offers the colours of Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music and YouTube Music as presets. ([#2848](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2848))

## Improved
- **AutoMix**: with crossfade on Auto, a fade now ends where the outgoing song's last chorus ends, and the next song comes in at the end of its intro. Both are read from the songs' own loudness. ([#2849](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2849))
- **Downloads**: a downloaded song's menu now says Remove download, and one still downloading says Cancel download. Several downloads can be removed at once from the Downloaded list's multi-select, and a removed or cancelled download goes back to not downloaded right away. ([#2176](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2176), [#2709](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/pull/2709), [core#43](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/core/pull/43))
- **Discord login on the Full build**: you now log in on Discord's own page in your browser, and SimpMusic no longer reads your account token. If you used Discord Rich Presence before, log in again once. FOSS builds keep the token login. ([#2852](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2852))
- **Scrollbars on Desktop**: every scrolling page, the sidebar, the queues and the comments sheet have a vertical scrollbar you can drag. ([#2873](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2873))
- **Home on Android** fits more on screen, and rounded artwork uses the same corner everywhere in the app. ([#2873](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2873))
- **Artist page on Desktop**: the artist's name logo scales with the page. ([#2853](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2853))
- **Playback after YouTube updates**: both stream extractors were updated to their latest versions, with fixes for songs that failed to play when YouTube's client version could not be read or the current player could not be identified on the device. ([PipePipe bba8d844](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/PipePipeExtractor/commit/bba8d84453bb2beab4182e5d16721e25c739b777), [BravePipe 991b158f](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/BravePipeExtractor/commit/991b158fee64c95f340803d26dc5b938b4b1cd01))
- **Skipping through a queue**: only the songs Next will play are prepared ahead, and the ones you skipped past are released. ([core ab5f1ab](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/core/commit/ab5f1abffbf693a1c12e7b27beb81ed37a3fbad8))
