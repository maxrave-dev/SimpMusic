# SimpMusic 2.2.0

## New
- **Sign in on Desktop from your phone**: scan a QR code in the Android app to send your YouTube, Spotify, Discord and Last.fm sign-ins to the Desktop app over your network. ([#2533](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2533))
- **Landscape fullscreen lyrics**: artwork and controls on one side, lyrics on the other. On Desktop the lyrics fill the window, and they also open from the mini player artwork. ([#2525](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2525), [#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Lyrics timing offset**: move synced lyrics earlier or later to match Bluetooth delay (Settings → Lyrics). ([#2338](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2338))
- **Word-by-word lyrics**: dots through instrumental breaks and a light that travels along each word. ([#2526](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2526))
- **More on Home**: a section's "More" button opens its full list on a new Browse page. ([#2461](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2461))
- **Liked songs on artist pages**: see and play the songs you liked from that artist. ([#2524](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2524))
- **Preferred audio language**: videos with several audio tracks play the original, or the language you choose. ([#2545](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2545))
- **System equalizer on Android**: use your phone's equalizer instead of the built-in one. ([#2464](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2464))
- **Artist page**: turn the Canvas on or off, and share the artist. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Swedish and Croatian**: two new app languages, translated by the community. ([Crowdin](https://crowdin.com/project/simpmusic))
- **Follow me on X** from Settings → About. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))

## Improved
- **Mini player**: a new default style. The translucent bar option is removed. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Artist pages**: the full description with working links, a dimmed and blurred header, and a landscape header shaped like the banner. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Grids fit the window**: artist albums and library playlists adapt their columns to the window width. ([#2528](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2528))
- **Desktop**: scrollbars on the Home grids that snap rows into place, and rows that stay readable on wide windows. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Long radio sessions**: old history is trimmed so the queue stays smooth. ([#2504](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2504))
- **Radio plays songs, not music videos**: when a song has an audio version, radio plays it instead of the video. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Resume where you left off**: the queue is always saved and resumes at the saved position. ([#2527](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2527))
- **Search**: tap the Search tab again to open the keyboard. ([#2348](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/pull/2348))
- **Credits**: legal disclaimer and the current copyright year. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
- **Unofficial copies**: repackaged or re-signed copies of the Android app are blocked. ([#2552](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2552))
