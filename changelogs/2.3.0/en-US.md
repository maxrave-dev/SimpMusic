# SimpMusic 2.3.0

## New
- **Apple Music player, redesigned**: the whole artwork without cropping, animated artwork in a frame of its own shape, a background made from the artwork's colours, the current lyric line above the seek bar, the stream's bitrate and sample rate, and an output button with Cast beside Listen Together. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Apple Music-style lyrics**: when the sung line changes, the lines move up one after another, the way Apple Music does, instead of as one block. ([#2408](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2408))
- **Home, redesigned**: "Your daily discover" as a large carousel, "Play all" on the sections that offer it, a play button on hover on Desktop, and new Library cards for Favorite, Followed, Most played and Downloaded that show your latest items. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Listening receipt**: share the songs you played most in a period as a printed receipt, from the Analytics screen. ([#2598](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2598))
- **What's your music taste?**: an AI card in Library reads your last 12 months of listening and describes your taste, using the AI provider you set up in Settings. ([#2568](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2568))
- **Audio output**: choose the speaker, headphones or Cast device right from the player, in every player style. Only SimpMusic's audio moves. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Live broadcasts**: YouTube Music's live stations play, with a LIVE badge on their cards. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Animated artwork on artist pages**: artist pages play the artist's animated artwork from Apple Music. ([#2579](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2579))
- **App log**: Settings → Developer option → App log shows the app's log. Export it to attach to a bug report, with keys, cookies and IP addresses removed. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Song options in the queue**: the ⋯ on a queue row opens the full song menu, with move up, move down, move to play next and remove. ([#2599](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2599), [#2542](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2542))
- **SimpMusic for vivo**: a separate APK that vivo's Origin Island and music widget can follow. ([#2074](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2074), [#2316](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2316))

## Improved
- **Classic and Material 3 Expressive players**: animated album artwork plays the way it does in the Apple Music player, and Material 3 Expressive takes its colours from the artwork. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Settings**: sections are grouped into cards. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Lyrics timing offset**: adjust it right from the lyrics screens, not only in Settings. ([#2338](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2338))
- **Windows**: the standard Windows title bar with its own minimize, maximize and close buttons. ([#2075](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2075), [#2597](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2597))
- **Images**: artwork is kept for a day and still shows when you are offline. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Downloads**: only the audio is downloaded while video playback is off. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Smoother UI**: the bottom bar, the mini player and the glass effects redraw less while nothing on screen changes. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
- **Developer blog**: the blog and its notifications now come from simpmusic.org. ([#2600](https://github.com/maxrave-dev/SimpMusic/issues/2600))
